Why it works

Built on how ads actually get tested.

The Studio follows one method from the first frame to the test plan.

  • The hook does the work. Most viewers decide in a second or two, so the first line gets the most care.
  • Your real product. Your actual screens, never a mock-up that promises more than the app does.
  • One change at a time. Every video shares the same body, so the result tells you which hook won.
  • Motion on frame one. The phone is already swiping when the ad starts, so the first second isn't wasted.
  • Made for sound off. Every word is on screen, highlighted as it's spoken.
The claim check

Every hook is checked against your brief before it can go in an ad:

Track where your money's been going, 10x faster.
  • Uses a number ("10x") that isn't in your facts.
  • Sounds like the app sees the viewer's spending. Make sure it really does, or reword.

  • Uses another brand's name. Fine as a comparison only: no logos or styling.

Questions

Before you start.

Which apps does it work for?

Phone apps (iPhone and Android), web apps and SaaS, anything you can show on a phone screen. Upload screenshots from a phone or simulator, or a short screen recording.

Do you run the ads for me?

No. The Studio makes the ads; you upload them to Meta, TikTok, YouTube or Snap yourself. It never asks for access to your ad accounts.

Who owns the ads?

You do. You're responsible for having the rights to the screens you upload and for what your ads claim.

Is the voice AI?

Yes. Voiceovers are generated with ElevenLabs, from a choice of eight voices. Each one is checked to make sure your app's name is said right, but listen to every ad before you run it.

Will my ads be approved?

The claim check catches common rejection reasons, such as invented numbers, implied access to the viewer's data and wording about personal attributes. But only the platform decides, and some categories (finance, health, dating, gambling, apps for children) have extra rules. The Studio flags those.

Does it write the hooks for me?

It gives you proven hook patterns and checks every hook you write. AI hook suggestions are being switched on during the beta.

How long does it take?

A preview of every scene takes seconds once it starts. A full pack takes a few minutes. During the beta, jobs can wait a few minutes in the queue; you can close the tab and come back.

What happens to my screenshots?

They're stored privately in your account, used only to make your ads, and deleted when you ask. See the privacy policy.
