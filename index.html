Questions

Before you sign up.

Will I always get 200 leads?

No. 200 is the monthly limit, not a promise. One search looks through one city and trade, and can return just a handful of leads that pass the quality gate. Reaching 200 usually takes many searches across cities and verticals. Only delivered leads count toward your limit.

Are the leads exclusive to me?

No. It's software that searches public ads, so another customer searching the same city and vertical can find the same business.

How long does a search take?

A search usually starts within 30 minutes of queuing it, then takes a few minutes to around half an hour depending on size. It keeps running if you close the tab.

Where does the data come from?

The public Meta Ad Library, the advertiser's public Facebook Page, their website, and public search results. It only uses contact details the business published. The engine only looks: it never logs in, fills in a form, books, calls or messages anyone.

How accurate is it?

It checks every ad at its own link and shows you its evidence, and a filter removes findings it can't support. Automated checks can still be wrong, and ads change, so open the proof before you quote it to a prospect. You can mark any finding as wrong.

Does it connect to my CRM?

Through a webhook or our API. Anything that accepts a webhook works, including Zapier and Make, which connect to most CRMs. There are no one-click direct integrations yet.

What about email rules?

You're the sender, so following the law is on you. In the US, CAN-SPAM applies to business email too. The Outreach plan adds your postal address and an unsubscribe link to every message and stops you emailing anyone who opted out. Cold marketing texts aren't allowed under our terms.

Are you connected to Meta?

No. ForgeLabs isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Meta Platforms.
