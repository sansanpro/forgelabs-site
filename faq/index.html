FAQ

Questions and answers

ForgeLabs App Ad Studio turns an app's screenshots into ready-to-test vertical video ads for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Here's everything people ask before they start.

The basics

Can I make a video ad from my app's screenshots?

Yes, that's what the Studio does. Upload your screenshots or a short screen recording, or paste your App Store or Google Play link to fill them in. Describe the app in one line and pick your hooks. It renders vertical video ads with your real screens in a phone frame, an AI voiceover and word-by-word captions.

Do I need a video editor?

No. Every ad is rendered from your screens and script, timed to the voice. You review a free preview of every scene before the full render, and you edit words, not a timeline.

Does it use AI actors or avatars?

No. Every ad shows your real app, not actors or generated footage. The voice is AI.

Which apps does it work for?

iPhone and Android apps, web apps and SaaS: anything you can show on a phone screen.

How long does it take?

A preview of every scene takes seconds once it starts, and a full pack takes a few minutes. During the beta, jobs can wait a few minutes in the queue; you can close the tab and come back.

Making the ads

What can I upload?

PNG, JPEG or WebP screenshots and MP4 or MOV screen recordings, up to 50 MB each and 30 files per app. Keep recordings under 30 seconds. You need at least two screens. Plain screenshots work better than captioned App Store ones, because the ad adds its own text.

Can I start from my App Store or Google Play link?

Yes. Paste the link and the Studio fills in the name, the one-liner, the icon and up to six phone screenshots from the public listing.

Does it write the hooks for me?

Yes. It reads your screenshots and suggests hooks in several proven types, such as comparison, problem, callout and values, then rewrites or drops lines that fail the two-second clarity test. You can write your own too. See hook examples for the types.

What's the claim check?

Every hook is checked before it can go in an ad: invented numbers, promises no app can keep, wording that implies the app can see the viewer's data, and wording about personal attributes such as health or finances, which ad platforms reject. Another brand's name passes as a comparison, without its logo.

Can I edit the script?

Yes. The body is two to five short lines, each paired with one of your screens. Change the words or the screen for any line, then preview before you render.

Is the voice AI?

Yes. Voiceovers are made with ElevenLabs, from a choice of eight voices. Each take is checked to make sure your app's name is said right, but listen to every ad before you run it.

Does it add music or sound effects?

Sound effects are optional and off unless you switch them on. Text types out on screen and the camera moves through each scene either way.

The pack

What's in the pack?

One zip with:

  • A 1080×1920 video for each hook, one to five, voiced, with captions burned in
  • A 4:5 and a 9:16 image for each hook
  • An .srt caption file for each video
  • Primary text and a headline for each ad
  • A test plan: how to run the hooks against each other and read the result

Why do all the videos share one body?

So a test compares only the hooks. If the videos differ in more than one place, you can't tell which change won.

Which platforms are the ads for?

Instagram and Facebook Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Snapchat. Text stays out of the areas where the platforms draw their own buttons and captions.

Running the ads

Do you run the ads for me?

No. You upload the ads to Meta, TikTok, YouTube or Snap yourself. The Studio never asks for access to your ad accounts.

Will my ads be approved?

The claim check catches common rejection reasons, but only the platform decides, and some categories (finance, health, dating, gambling, apps for children) have extra rules. The Studio flags those.

What results should I expect?

No one can promise numbers for your app. One real test is public: StockMatcherAI's ads made with this engine got 4.6% link click-through on Meta over 7,401 impressions, at $0.43 a click. The case study has the details, including what it doesn't show.

How should I test the ads?

Run three to five hooks at once with the same targeting, give each about 1,000 impressions, don't edit them mid-test, and compare click-through between your own ads. The guide covers it.

Pricing, ownership and data

How much does it cost?

It's free during the beta: one test pack of up to five videos, and previews are always free. Paid plans with a monthly pack allowance are coming.

Who owns the ads?

You do. You're responsible for having the rights to the screens you upload and for what your ads claim.

What happens to my screenshots?

They're stored privately in your account, used only to make your ads, and deleted when you ask. See the privacy policy.
