Examples

App video ad examples

Three real ads for StockMatcherAI, an iPhone app that matches people with companies working on causes they care about. Each was made from the app's own screens, with no actors and no mock-ups, and ran on Facebook and Instagram. Here's each one with its script, how it's built and what it got.

By Hassan Omar · 20 November 2026 · Results from 5 to 10 November 2026

1. "It's like Tinder, but for stocks."

19 seconds, 1080×1920

5.8%link click-through
1,784impressions
$0.39per link click

Hook type: comparison.

Script: It's like Tinder, but for stocks. Swipe right to like. Swipe left to pass. Every card shows what a company does, the proof, and the things to know. Then see what your likes say about you. Download StockMatcherAI free on the App Store.

Why it works: it explains the whole app in six words, because everyone already knows how Tinder works. The body then shows exactly that: a swipe right, a swipe left, a company card. It was the plainest hook in the test, and of the ads with over 1,000 impressions it had the highest click-through and the cheapest clicks.

2. "It's Tinder, except the matches have revenue."

21 seconds, 1080×1920

Hook type: comparison, with a twist.

Script: It's Tinder, except the matches have revenue. Swipe right on real companies working on causes you pick. Every card shows what it does, the proof, and what to know. Stuck on something? Ask AI. Then see what your likes say about you. Download StockMatcherAI free on the App Store.

Why it works: the same comparison, with a joke that still says what the app is. A hand is already mid-swipe on the first frame, so there's movement before the viewer has decided whether to stay.

3. "You'd donate to fight cancer. Could you name one company working on it?"

22 seconds, 1080×1920

3.4%link click-through
1,733impressions
$0.53per link click

Hook type: values.

Script: You'd donate to fight cancer. Could you name one company working on it? Pick the causes you care about, like beating cancer, clean energy, or clean water. See real companies working on them, with proof and a source you can check. Then you decide. Download StockMatcherAI free on the App Store.

Why it works: it starts from something the viewer cares about and opens a gap most people can't fill. It needs a second to land, where the comparison lands at once, which may be why it trailed. It also carries the disclaimer the app's finance category needs.

What all three have in common

PartLengthWhat's on screen
Hook2–4 sThe line in large type, word by word as it's spoken, over the app already moving.
Body10–15 sShort lines, each over the screen that does exactly that: the swipe, the company card, the proof and its source.
Call to action3–4 s"Download StockMatcherAI free on the App Store", said and shown.
  • The real app, in a phone frame. Nothing on screen promises more than the app does.
  • Every word captioned. Many people watch with the sound off, so the captions carry the ad alone.
  • One body per test. Ads in the same round share the body, so the results compare only the hooks.
  • Text inside the safe zone. Nothing sits under the buttons and captions the platforms draw on top.

More hooks, including the ones that lost, are on the hook examples page. The whole campaign, all 14 ads and the spend, is in the StockMatcherAI case study. These are link clicks, not installs, from one test of about $150; they rank hooks against each other, not promise results for another app.
