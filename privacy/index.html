Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Our customers

We store your account email, password (hashed), plan, company details, templates, searches and leads, and an encrypted copy of any mailbox password you connect. Payments are handled by Stripe, and we don't see your card number. We use your data to run the service and contact you about your account. We don't sell it.

Businesses in our results

The service reports on businesses that advertise publicly. It collects public ad content and contact details the business published, such as a business email or phone number on its website or Facebook Page. If a person's name appears, it's because it was published or is part of a published business email address.

To be left out of future searches, use the removal form. To stop email from one of our customers, use the unsubscribe link in that email.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database and hosting of app data), GitHub (running the search engine and hosting this site), Firecrawl (fetching public web pages) and Stripe (payments). Each handles data only to provide its service.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep account data while your account is open. To delete your account and its data, email hassan@forgelaboratory.net.
