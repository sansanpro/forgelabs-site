Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

What we store

Your account email and password (hashed by our sign-in provider), and what you put into the Studio: your apps' briefs, the screenshots, recordings and icons you upload, your hooks and scripts, and the ads made from them. We also count your packs and AI requests to apply the beta's limits.

How we use it

Only to run the Studio and to contact you about your account. We don't sell it, and we don't use your uploads or ads to train AI models.

Service providers

Each handles data only to provide its service:

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and private file storage.
  • ElevenLabs: voiceovers. It receives the words of your ad scripts.
  • Anthropic: hook suggestions, claim checks and script drafts. It receives your brief, hooks and screen labels.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

Your ads are rendered on our own rendering machine, which downloads your uploads for the job and deletes its working copy when the job ends.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account is open. You can delete uploads in the Studio at any time. To delete your account and everything in it, email hassan@forgelaboratory.net.

Our earlier product

ForgeLabs previously offered lead software for agencies, which reported on businesses running public ads using contact details those businesses published. It's no longer offered. To be left out of its records, use the removal form. To stop email from one of its former customers, use the unsubscribe link in that email.
