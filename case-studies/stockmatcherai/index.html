StockMatcherAI's video ads on Meta

StockMatcherAI is an iPhone app that matches people with companies working on causes they care about, one swipe at a time. Its ads were made with the engine behind ForgeLabs App Ad Studio, from the app's real screens, and ran on Facebook and Instagram.

What was made

Fourteen ads, all from the same app screens and one British voice:

  • Nine ads in the first round (3 videos and 6 images), across three hook types: gamified comparisons, plain-English education, and values.
  • Five new videos on day two, replacing the five images that had 0 or 1 clicks each.

Every video in a round shares one body, so the results compare only the hooks. The body shows the app working straight after the hook: swiping, the company card, the proof and its source.

How it ran

  • Objective: Traffic, to a one-screen download page that links to the App Store.
  • Three ad sets, one per hook type, at $8.33 a day each.
  • Audience: US, iPhones on iOS 15 or later, Meta's broad Advantage+ audience. The app is declared under Meta's financial products and services category, which fixes the age range at 18 and over.
  • Placements: Facebook, Instagram and Threads.

What it showed

The plainest hook won

"It's like Tinder, but for stocks" explains the whole app in six words. Of the ads with over 1,000 impressions, it had the highest click-through and the cheapest clicks, and it got the most clicks of any ad.

Abstract hooks lost

"Your money is already saying something. Is it what you believe?" got 1 click from 79 impressions. It sounds good but never says what the app is. It was among the first ads replaced.

Video took almost everything

The eight video ads took 97% of the spend and 334 of the 342 clicks. Meta's delivery favoured them from the first day, which is why every replacement was a video.

A new hook can still beat the leader

"Analysis paralysis isn't research" joined on day two and reached 6.0% click-through at $0.31 a click over 766 impressions, better than the leader so far, though on fewer impressions.

What this doesn't show

  • Installs. A Traffic campaign without Meta's SDK in the app can't see installs, so they're counted in App Store Connect and aren't included here.
  • A long-run result. This is under a week and about $150. It's enough to rank hooks against each other, not to promise any other app the same numbers.
